Dr. Donny Walker is the Principal Investigator of the Microbiome Ecology Lab at Middle Tennessee State University. His research focuses on understanding the interactions between wildlife hosts, their microbiomes, and emerging infectious fungal pathogens.

**Education:**
- Ph.D. Rutgers University (2012)
- B.S. SUNY College of Environmental Science and Forestry (2007)
- A.S. SUNY Niagara (2005)

Dr. Walker's lab investigates pathogen-induced dysbiosis and bacterial-fungal interactions across multiple ecological scales, with implications for disease ecology and biodiversity conservation.
